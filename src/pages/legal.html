Privacy notice

This notice explains what Zeroing does with the details you give us through this site, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

What we collect

  • The join form: brand name, founder name, city, year started, what you make, Instagram, website if given, your answers, email and phone if given.
  • The suggest a guest form: the guest's name, work and a public link, your reason, your name and email.
  • The contact and research forms: your name, email and message.
  • The letter: your email address.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics on this site. Three outside services are involved when you visit:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores form entries.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Spotify serves the episode players on the episode page. Spotify may set its own cookies when a player loads or plays, under its privacy policy.

The forms are for people aged 18 and over.

Why we collect it

Only for the purpose named on each form: to review an application, consider a guest, reply to a message, follow up a research lead, or send the letter. We do not sell your details. We do not share them outside Zeroing, except with the services above that run the site, or where the law requires it.

If you suggest a guest, you are telling us about another person. Share only what they have made public themselves. We use it only to decide whether to contact them, and we tell them how we found them when we do.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Form entries are stored in a database run on Cloudflare, which hosts this site. We keep an entry only as long as we need it for its purpose. Some records and logs may be kept for at least one year where the law requires it. We delete form entries 12 months after we last use them for their purpose.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you have sent us, or withdraw your consent, at any time. You can also name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before you withdrew it. Write to the grievance contact below. Every letter has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave the list at any time.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is able to hear complaints under the Act.
