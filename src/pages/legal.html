Privacy notice

This notice explains what Zeroing does with the details you give us through this site, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

What we collect

  • The join form: brand name, founder name, city, year started, what you make, Instagram, website if given, your answers, email and phone if given.
  • The suggest a guest form: the guest's name, work and a public link, your reason, your name and email.
  • The contact and research forms: your name, email and message.
  • The letter: your email address.

We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

Only for the purpose named on each form: to review an application, consider a guest, reply to a message, follow up a research lead, or send the letter. We do not sell or share your details with anyone outside Zeroing.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Form entries are stored in a database run on Cloudflare, which hosts this site. We keep an entry only as long as we need it for its purpose. To confirm: a fixed period, for example 24 months, after which entries are deleted.
