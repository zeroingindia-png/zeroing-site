The conversation

She left a corporate PR career at 21 with no film school behind her. For ten years she did every job except direct: she made coffee, carried tripods and logged footage. Then she spent two decades filming the animals few channels commission, from the gharial and the Indian wolf to the red panda and the dugong.

In this conversation she goes back to where it began, and to what the work has actually taken.

Films
Over 100, across blue chip, science, drama and conservation
Awards
Four National Film Awards, among more than 275 awards and nominations
Series
On the Brink, on lesser known endangered species
Night filming
Described as the first filmmaker in India given government permission to shoot inside a national park after sunset
Women of the Wild
Has profiled over 400 women in science, ecology and conservation, across India, Pakistan and Malaysia
