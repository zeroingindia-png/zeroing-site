Why Zeroing exists

India runs on small businesses and on people whose work is never written down. These are the numbers we started from.

  1. ianslive.in
    8.7 crorebusinesses registered on Udyam and the Udyam Assist Platform, June 2026

    Together they make about 31% of India's GDP and almost half of its exports, and employ 38.9 crore people.

    Source: official MSME fact sheet, reported by IANS, 26 June 2026

  2. india.entrepreneur.com
    13 in 100use social media to market themselves

    Seven in ten still depend on word of mouth and traditional selling. Many find it hard to sell beyond their own town, even as getting credit stays a challenge.

    Source: SIDBI, Understanding Indian MSME Sector: Progress and Challenges, May 2025, a survey of over 2,000 businesses, as reported by Entrepreneur India

  3. dataforindia.com
    9 in 10Indian workers work informally

    Fewer than one in ten has a job contract longer than a year, and about one in ten has any social security. Most of the country's working life leaves no record.

    Source: Data For India, from the Periodic Labour Force Survey 2024

  4. inderscience.com
    Togethersmall firms get better at their craft

    A study of two foundry clusters in South India found that firms which cooperated, with each other and along their supply chain, built significantly stronger technical capability.

    Source: Raghavendra and Bala Subrahmanya, International Journal of Management and Enterprise Development, 2005
