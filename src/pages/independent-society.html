Why it exists

India keeps a record of what it decided. It keeps far less of how the work got done.

Nine in ten Indian workers work informally, with no contract and often no paper trail. We sit with the people who did the work, and with those who chose a job outside the list every Indian family knows by heart.

We listen long enough for the polished version to run out. What is left is the first salary, the rent, the years nobody filmed. A record, not a show.

How a conversation works

  1. We research for weeks, so the guest never has to explain the basics.
  2. We film where they work or live, in a single unhurried day.
  3. We ask about the years, not the awards.
  4. We close with The Other Details. The money, the permissions, the equipment. The part every feature cuts.
