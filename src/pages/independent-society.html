Independent Society

A podcast where one person at a time has a long honest conversation about what their professional life in India actually looked like from the inside, with no highlights or performance.

Why it exists

India has records of what it decided. It has far fewer of how the work actually got done.

Nine in ten Indian workers work informally, with no contract and often no paper trail. Independent Society sits with the people who did the work, and with people who chose a profession outside the list every Indian family knows by heart.

Every episode is a record, not entertainment.

How a conversation works

  1. We research for weeks, so the guest never has to explain the basics.
  2. We film where they work or live, in a single unhurried day.
  3. We ask about the years, not the awards.
  4. We close with The Other Details: the money, the permissions, the equipment. The part every feature cuts.
