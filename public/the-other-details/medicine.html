Study 04 · Medicine

What is in the price. Medicine in India.

The same medicine can cost many times more with a brand name on it. We followed the printed price from the strip to the hospital bill.

Watch the three reels on YouTube ↗How we check data
What we found

Five numbers. Each one sourced.

₹229 vs ₹18.70

for a strip of rosuvastatin 10 mg, a common cholesterol medicine: branded against Jan Aushadhi.

82%

of the medicine market by value has no price ceiling. Companies may raise the printed price by up to 10% a year.

20,149

Jan Aushadhi Kendras now sell low-cost generic medicines across India.

43%

of health spending in India is still paid by families from their own pocket, down from 64% in 2013-14.

₹24,000 vs ₹1,100

is the typical out-of-pocket cost of a hospital stay: private against government.

Why it matters

The price on the strip is not a fixed cost of the medicine. Knowing which part of it is real helps a family choose, ask and save.
