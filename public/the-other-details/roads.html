Study 03 · Roads

Every three minutes. Who dies on India's roads.

India's own road crash report, read line by line: who is killed, at what hour, and why.

Watch the reel on YouTube ↗How we check data
What we found

Five numbers. Each one sourced.

485

people die on India's roads every day. That is about one every three minutes.

46%

of the people killed were riding two-wheelers.

2 in 3

of those killed were aged 18 to 45, the years people earn and raise families.

6 to 9 pm

are the deadliest hours of the day.

~70%

of road deaths were linked to over-speeding.

Why it matters

Most road deaths are not random. They cluster by vehicle, by age, by hour and by speed, which means they can be planned against.

Sources
  • Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Road Accidents in India 2024
  • As reported by ThePrint and PolicyEdge
