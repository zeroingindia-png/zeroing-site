How the lab works

Data can be wrong. We check anyway.

The same six rules run every study, from the first search to the last frame of the reel.

01

Trace to the origin

Every figure goes back to the report that first published it, not the article that repeated it.

02

Check the date

Older than about two years, or arithmetic that does not reconcile: labelled or replaced.

03

Ranges stay ranges

Forecasts are dashed, estimates show their spread, "more than" keeps its open end.

04

One chart per job

Squares for "times", unit grids for shares, lines for time, fans for forecasts, maps for place.

05

Fair and timeless

No sides taken, no seasons named, nobody mocked. The data leads.

06

Credit the owners

Data belongs to its original sources and owners. The Other Details only presents it.

Every figure is traced to the report that first published it and marked with a verdict. Current: primary source, recent. Outdated: older than about two years and still widely quoted. Check: reported second-hand or does not reconcile. Excluded: no named or reliable source.
