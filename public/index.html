Why Zeroing exists

India runs on small businesses and on people whose work is never written down. These are the numbers we started from.

  1. 8.7 croresmall businesses registered on Udyam, June 2026

    Together they make about 31% of India's GDP and almost half of its exports, and employ 38.9 crore people.

    Source: official MSME fact sheet, reported by IANS, 26 June 2026

  2. 13 in 100use social media to find customers

    Seven in ten still depend on word of mouth and traditional selling. Reaching buyers beyond their own town is a bigger problem for them than getting a loan.

    Source: SIDBI, Understanding Indian MSME Sector, 2025, a survey of over 2,000 businesses

  3. 9 in 10Indian workers work informally

    Fewer than one in ten has a job contract longer than a year, and about one in ten has any social security. Most of the country's working life leaves no record.

    Source: Data For India, from the Periodic Labour Force Survey 2024

  4. Togethersmall firms get better at their craft

    A study of two foundry clusters in South India found that firms which cooperated, with each other and along their supply chain, built significantly stronger technical capability.

    Source: Raghavendra and Bala Subrahmanya, International Journal of Management and Enterprise Development, 2005

So Zeroing does two things. We help Indian brands find each other's customers, and we keep an honest record of how work here actually gets done.

How we work

Two simple routines, run the same way every time.

With brands

  1. You apply. Tell us what you make, where, and one door you could open for another brand.
  2. We meet the founder. A short call, so both sides know it fits.
  3. We introduce you. To brands whose customers, suppliers or stalls overlap with yours.
  4. We grow in public. Brands present Independent Society together and are credited on every episode.

With the record

  1. We research for weeks, so the guest never has to explain the basics.
  2. We film where they work or live, in a single unhurried day.
  3. We ask about the years, not the awards.
  4. We write down The Other Details: the money, the permissions, the paperwork. With sources.
